运费试算器Estimate your freight

寄之前先算一算要花多少钱？这里两个小工具够用：算运费、查免费派送区域。算出来的数字是预估，最终以仓库复重与客服确认为准。 Want to work out the cost before you ship? Two quick tools: estimate the freight and check whether your area gets free delivery. Estimates only — the final figure follows the warehouse re-weigh and our confirmation.

工具一 · 运费试算Tool 1 · Cost estimator

填箱规和重量，先算出大概多少钱Enter carton size and weight for a rough cost

计费按实际重量与体积重取大者。体积重 = 长 × 宽 × 高 ÷ 6000（厘米制），所以轻飘飘的大箱子，往往按体积重收费。 We charge on the greater of actual weight and volumetric weight. Volumetric weight = L × W × H ÷ 6000 (in cm), so a big but light carton is usually billed on volume.

空运还是海运？一张表看完Air or sea — the whole comparison

急件、样品、小件补货走空运；大件、重货、不急的走海运。两边都含双清包税与送货到门。 Urgent parcels, samples and small restocks go by air. Bulky, heavy or non-urgent cargo goes by sea. Both include customs clearance, tax and door delivery.
