常见问题Frequently asked questions

关于价格与计费、时效与航班、派送与地址、保险与赔付、禁运与包装，客户问得最多的 20 个问题都在这里。没找到答案的，直接问客服。 The twenty questions we hear most — pricing and chargeable weight, transit and schedules, delivery and addresses, insurance and claims, prohibited goods and packing. Anything missing, just ask.

计费与价格Pricing & charging

空运 420P/KG，约 3 天到菲律宾。海运按重量阶梯计价：1–30KG 为 150P/KG，30–50KG 为 130P/KG，50KG 以上为 100P/KG，约 2 周（14 天）到菲律宾。两种方式都是一公斤起运、送货到门、双清包税。 Air is 420 PHP/kg, arriving in about 3 days. Sea is tiered by weight: 150 PHP/kg for 1–30 kg, 130 PHP/kg for 30–50 kg and 100 PHP/kg above 50 kg, arriving in about 2 weeks. Both start from 1 kg, are delivered to the door, and are customs cleared with tax paid.

以上均以菲律宾比索（P）计价，最终以客服确认为准。 All rates are in Philippine Peso (PHP) and subject to confirmation by our team.

最低按 1 公斤计费，不足 1 公斤也按 1 公斤算。小件、散货同样送货到门，我们没有设置「大货才接」的门槛，寄一公斤的东西也一样送上门。 The minimum charge is one kilogram; anything lighter is still billed as 1 kg. Small parcels get the same door delivery — there is no bulk-only threshold.

计费重量取实际重量与体积重量中的较大值。体积重量计算公式为：长 × 宽 × 高（CM）÷ 6000。举例：60 × 40 × 50 CM 的箱子，体积重为 20KG；若实重只有 12KG，仍按 20KG 计费。 Chargeable weight is the greater of actual weight and volumetric weight. Volumetric weight = L × W × H (cm) ÷ 6000. Example: a 60 × 40 × 50 cm carton has a volumetric weight of 20 kg — even if it actually weighs 12 kg, you are billed for 20 kg.

除运费外，只有两项费用可能发生且都会提前说明：保价费（按物品价值 5%）与非免费区域的打车配送车费（到付、实报实销）。没有清关费、关税或派送附加费。 Beyond freight, only two charges can ever arise, and both are disclosed up front: the insurance premium (5% of declared value) and the taxi delivery fare outside our free zones (paid on delivery, at cost). There are no clearance fees, duties or delivery surcharges.

在免费派送区域内不需要再付任何费用——运费已经包含中国出口报关与菲律宾进口清关，这就是「双清包税」。免费派送区域为 MAKATI、PASAY、BGC、MANDALUYONG、PARANAQUE 五个区域。 Within our free delivery zones the receiver pays nothing extra — the freight already includes both China export clearance and Philippines import customs. Those zones are MAKATI, PASAY, BGC, MANDALUYONG and PARANAQUE.

海运按阶梯定价：货量越大，单位操作与舱位成本被摊得越薄，所以 30KG 以上单价降到 130P/KG，50KG 以上降到 100P/KG。不急的大货走海运最省，急件与轻小件走空运更合适。 Sea freight is tiered: the bigger the shipment, the thinner the per-unit handling and slot cost. Above 30 kg the rate drops to 130 PHP/kg and above 50 kg to 100 PHP/kg. Bulky, non-urgent cargo is cheapest by sea; urgent or light parcels suit air.

时效与航班Transit & schedules

约 3 天到菲律宾。我们一周有六次航班，货物入仓后即可排仓发运。 About 3 days to the Philippines. We operate six flights a week, so cargo is scheduled as soon as it reaches our warehouse.

约 2 周（14 天）到菲律宾，船期固定在每周三发货。想赶同一个船期，请提前把货送到我们的中国转运仓。 About 2 weeks to the Philippines, with a fixed Wednesday sailing. To catch a given sailing, get your goods to our China warehouse in good time.

从中国仓签收货物的时间起算，而不是从您下单或付款的时间起算。也就是说，国内快递在路上跑的时间不计入时效，货到我们仓里之后才开始计时。 It starts when our China warehouse takes receipt of your goods, not when you place the order or pay. Time spent in domestic transit is not counted — the clock begins once the cargo is with us.

中国与菲律宾的法定节假日、以及台风季等不可抗力因素，都可能影响航班起降、清关与末端派送。遇到这类情况，客服会提前告知并同步最新进度，不会让您被动等待。 Public holidays in China and the Philippines, plus typhoon season and other force-majeure events, can affect flights, customs clearance and last-mile delivery. When this happens, our team tells you in advance and shares the latest status — you will never be left guessing.

派送与地址Delivery & addresses

以下 5 个区域免费配送上门：MAKATI、PASAY、BGC、MANDALUYONG、PARANAQUE。 Free door delivery in these five areas: MAKATI, PASAY, BGC, MANDALUYONG and PARANAQUE.

免费区域之外采用打车配送，车费到付，实报实销。我们按实际车费收取，不在车费上加价。偏远地区也可以转由菲律宾当地快递派送，具体请联系客服确认。 Outside the free zones we deliver by taxi/ride-hailing with the fare paid on delivery, charged at actual cost with no mark-up. For remote areas we can also hand over to a local Philippine courier — ask us for details.

地址信息必须填写准确，这是最容易出问题的一环。建议按下表核对： Address details must be accurate — this is where most problems start. Check against this list:

  • 区域名称（如 MAKATI、BGC）District name (e.g. MAKATI, BGC)
  • 街道、门牌号、楼栋与单元号Street, number, building and unit
  • 收件人姓名与可接通的手机号Receiver name and a reachable mobile number
  • 地址结尾备注清楚代收人（前台 / 门卫 / 同事）Note at the end who receives it (reception, guard, colleague)
  • 代码编号（会员号）务必填写，这是仓库识别货物归属的唯一依据The code number (member ID) is mandatory — the only way to match your parcel

可以。请在地址结尾备注清楚代收人的姓名与联系方式，例如「由前台 Mary 代收」「放在 XX 楼门卫处」。这样派送员到达时不会因为找不到你本人而白跑一趟。 Yes. Just note the receiver's name and contact at the end of the address, e.g. "received by Mary at reception" or "leave with the guard at Tower XX". That way our rider is not sent away empty-handed.

保险与赔付Insurance & claims

保费按物品价值的 5% 收取。请如实申报物品名称与价值，投保后一旦发生丢失，按保价金额赔付。电子产品、高价值样品与品牌货建议都投保。 The premium is 5% of the declared goods value. Declare the item name and value accurately; if the shipment is lost, we pay out the declared amount. We recommend this for electronics, high-value samples and branded goods.

未购买保险的货件，丢失赔偿3 倍运费，且最高不超过物品价值的 10%。举例：运费 1000P 的货件，未投保的赔付上限为 3000P，同时不能超过该物品价值的 10%。所以贵重物品强烈建议投保。 For uninsured shipments, compensation is 3× the freight, capped at 10% of the goods' value. Example: on 1,000 PHP of freight, the cap is 3,000 PHP — and never more than 10% of the item's value. For valuables, insure it.

投保前请提供物品名称、数量与申报价值；如遇异常，请保留货物照片、装箱清单、购物凭证或发票，并第一时间联系客服，理赔以申报价值为基准。具体流程与所需材料请在下单前与客服确认。 Before insuring, provide the item name, quantity and declared value. If something goes wrong, keep photos, the packing list and purchase receipts or invoices, and contact us immediately — the claim is based on the declared value. Confirm the exact process with our team before you ship.

禁运与包装Restrictions & packing

国家明令禁止的违禁品一律不承运，常见类别包括： We never carry items prohibited by law. Common categories include:

  • 易燃、易爆、腐蚀性及危险化学品Flammable, explosive, corrosive and hazardous chemicals
  • 枪支、弹药及管制刀具Firearms, ammunition and controlled blades
  • 毒品及违禁药品Narcotics and prohibited drugs
  • 活体动植物、种子与土壤Live animals and plants, seeds and soil
  • 货币、有价证券与贵金属原材Currency, securities and raw precious metals

这类属于受限品类，需视品类与批次而定，不同渠道的限制不同，也受目的地清关政策影响。发货前请先与客服核对，确认能走再打包，避免货物在中途被扣或退运。 These are restricted categories that depend on the item and the batch — rules differ by channel and are affected by destination customs policy. Check with our team before packing so your goods are not held or returned mid-route.

包装上：纸箱优先于塑料袋，易碎品加缓冲材料，液体密封后再套防漏袋，箱体接缝处用胶带封边。 Packing: cartons beat bags, add padding for fragile items, seal liquids inside a leak-proof bag, and tape all carton seams.

省运费上：因为轻抛货可能按体积重计费，压掉多余体积就是直接省钱。去掉多余填充物、别用大而空的外箱、能合箱的合并起来，往往能显著降低计费重量。 On cost: because bulky-light cargo may be charged volumetrically, cutting volume cuts the bill directly. Remove excess filler, avoid oversized half-empty cartons and consolidate into fewer boxes — the chargeable weight often drops noticeably.
