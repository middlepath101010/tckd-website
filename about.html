关于同城快递About TCD Express

同城快递（TCD EXPRESS）是专注中国到菲律宾的专线物流服务商。我们在中国福建与广州设有收货转运仓，在菲律宾马尼拉设有自营仓与派送团队，把「中国收货 → 空运/海运 → 马尼拉分拣 → 送货到门」这一条链路全部握在自己手里。 TCD Express is a dedicated freight operator on the China–Philippines lane. We run consolidation warehouses in Fujian and Guangzhou, plus our own warehouse and delivery team in Manila — keeping the whole chain, from pickup in China to door delivery, in our own hands.

我们做什么What we do

中国到菲律宾，空运与海运双通道Air and sea, China to the Philippines

我们主做中国—菲律宾专线空运与海运，同时提供拼装打包、菲律宾当地派送与海外仓服务。面向在菲华人、华商、跨境电商卖家，以及与中国有货物往来的中小企业。 We specialise in air and sea freight on the China–Philippines lane, alongside consolidation, last-mile delivery in the Philippines and warehousing. Our customers are Chinese communities and businesses in the Philippines, cross-border sellers, and SMEs trading with China.

空运专线Air freight

一周六次航班，约 3 天到菲律宾，420P/KG。适合电商补货、急件与时效敏感货物。 Six flights a week, about 3 days to the Philippines, 420 PHP/kg. Ideal for restocking and urgent consignments.

海运专线Sea freight

每周三发货，约 2 周到菲律宾，100–150P/KG。重量越大单价越低，大宗货物可按立方计算，具体详谈。 Departs every Wednesday, about 2 weeks to the Philippines, 100–150 PHP/kg. Bulk cargo can be priced by cubic metre — talk to us.

海外仓与一件代发Warehousing & drop-shipping

整批货入马尼拉自营仓，按订单分地址单独派送。卖家不用在自己家里囤货，接单后由我们出货。 Store in bulk at our Manila warehouse, then we pick and deliver each order to a different address — no need to stock goods at home.

仓储网络Our network

两端都有我们自己的仓Our own warehouses at both ends

跨境最怕货在别人手上没人管。我们两端自营，从中国收货那一刻起，到菲律宾送货上门为止，中间每一环都由自己的团队负责。 The biggest risk in cross-border shipping is cargo sitting with a third party. We operate both ends ourselves — every step from intake in China to delivery in the Philippines is handled by our own team.

    双转运仓 + 自营仓网络成型Two China hubs plus our own PH warehouse

    福建仓、广州仓两处收货口，配合马尼拉自营仓与本地派送网络，实现一公斤起免费派送到门。Fujian and Guangzhou intake points, plus the Manila warehouse and local delivery network — free door delivery from one kilogram.

  4. 2026
    空运 3 天、海运 2 周Air in 3 days, sea in 2 weeks

    空运一周六次航班直达，海运每周三固定发货，配合双清包税到门，服务跨境电商与中小企业日常往来的稳定节奏。Six direct flights a week and a fixed Wednesday sailing, both customs cleared and tax paid to the door — a steady rhythm for cross-border sellers and SMEs.
