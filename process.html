计费规则Chargeable weight

计费重量怎么算：实重与体积重取较大值Chargeable weight: the greater of actual and volumetric

运费不看单一的重量。当货物体积重量大于实际重量时，以体积重量方式计算运费。轻泡货（体积大、重量小）尤其要提前算一下。 Freight is not based on weight alone. When the volumetric weight exceeds the actual weight, we charge by volumetric weight. Bulky-but-light cargo should always be calculated in advance.

体积重量计算公式Volumetric weight formula

体积重量（KG）= 长 × 宽 × 高（CM）÷ 6000Volumetric weight (kg) = L × W × H (cm) ÷ 6000

长宽高以厘米（CM）为单位，取纸箱的外尺寸；得出的结果单位为公斤。多箱货物逐箱计算后相加。 Measure in centimetres using the outer dimensions of the carton; the result is in kilograms. For multiple cartons, calculate each box and add them up.

想省运费，最直接的办法是压缩包装体积：去掉多余填充、拆掉大而空的外箱、能合箱的合并成一箱。 The quickest way to save: compress the volume — remove excess filler, avoid oversized half-empty cartons, and consolidate into fewer boxes.

保险与赔付规则Insurance and claims

贵重物品建议购买保险。保费按物品价值 5% 收取，购买后一旦发生丢失，按保价金额赔付。没有购买保险的货件，丢失赔偿 3 倍运费，且最高不超过物品价值的 10%。 We recommend insurance for valuable goods. The premium is 5% of the declared value, and if the shipment is lost we pay out the declared amount. For uninsured shipments, compensation is 3× the freight and never exceeds 10% of the goods' value.

注意事项Things to note

发货前的 6 项检查Six checks before you ship

地址必须准确Accurate address

区域、街道、门牌、单元号、代收人写全，地址结尾备注清楚。District, street, number, unit and receiver — plus a clear note at the end.

代码编号务必填写Always include the code number

编号是仓库识别货物归属的唯一依据，漏填会导致无法入库与派送。It is the only way to match a parcel to an owner; missing it blocks both intake and delivery.

时效从签收起算Transit starts at receipt

转运时间从中国仓签收货物那一刻开始计算，不是从下单时间算。Counting begins when our China warehouse receives the goods, not when the order is placed.

包装要抗压防潮Pack for pressure and moisture

纸箱优先于塑料袋，易碎品加缓冲，液体类密封后外套防漏袋。Prefer cartons over bags, add padding for fragile items, and seal liquids inside a leak-proof bag.

贵重品记得投保Insure valuables

保费为物品价值的 5%，未投保货件赔付上限为物品价值的 10%。Premium is 5% of value; uninsured payouts are capped at 10% of value.

违禁品不要寄No prohibited items

国家明令禁止的违禁品一律不承运，具体品类请先咨询客服确认。We do not carry items prohibited by law. Please confirm any questionable category with us first.

关于禁运与受限品类：易燃易爆品、枪支弹药、毒品、活体动植物、货币及有价证券等国家明令禁止的违禁品一律不予承运。食品、化妆品、电子产品等特殊品类需视批次与渠道确认，发货前请先与客服核对，避免货物在中途被扣。 Prohibited and restricted goods: flammables and explosives, firearms and ammunition, narcotics, live animals and plants, currency and securities, and anything else banned by law are never carried. Food, cosmetics and electronics depend on the batch and the channel, so check with our team before shipping to avoid seizure in transit.
