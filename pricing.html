计费口径How we charge

计费重量取实际重量与体积重量中的较大值。体积重量 ＝ 长 × 宽 × 高（CM）÷ 6000。轻抛货请务必提前算一下，避免运费超预算。 Chargeable weight is the greater of actual weight and volumetric weight. Volumetric weight = L × W × H (cm) ÷ 6000. Please check this for bulky light cargo to avoid surprises.
